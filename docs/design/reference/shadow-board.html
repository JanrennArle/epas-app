Find the fault

When the shop is short of equipment, the practice is still here. Every appliance below has a real fault waiting for you to track down.

  • Electric fan
  • Electric flat iron
  • Rechargeable LED lamp
  • CCTV camera channel
  • Fire alarm zone
  • Audio amplifier
  • Flat screen television
  • Motor control board

Plus a multimeter trainer, a power supply you build stage by stage, and seven parts and signal path exercises. 17 labs in all, and 8 performance tasks you carry out on real equipment.

Shop rules

  • {{ICON:WifiSlash:bold}}Works with no signal

    Open it once and everything runs on your phone after that. No data, no Wi-Fi.

  • {{ICON:DeviceMobile:bold}}Your work stays with you

    No account and nothing sent anywhere. You hand in your own results file.

  • {{ICON:Warning:fill}}Mains voltage can kill

    Every procedure says when the power is on and when it is off. Check with your teacher before you open a real appliance.
